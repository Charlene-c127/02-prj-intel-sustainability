Sustainability Through the Ages

Explore Intel's journey through time, discovering how Intel's commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

Global Accessibility and Localization

Intel is committed to creating digital experiences that support users across different languages, regions, and accessibility needs.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename the newly formed company NM Electronics to Intel Corporation.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor.

1978

8086 Processor

Intel launches the 8086 processor, establishing the x86 architecture.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel identifies a major emissions challenge and invests in cleaner manufacturing.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE strategy and 2030 goals.

2022

Net-Zero by 2040

Intel announces its commitment to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2040.

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit to support collaboration across the industry.
